Summary

    Data: NASA MERRA-2 monthly mean total column ozone (TO3), area-averaged over the selected region via NASA Giovanni, Jan 1980 – Sep 2026. Seasonal cycle removed by subtracting each calendar month’s long-term average. Trend: linear regression on monthly anomalies with an autocorrelation-adjusted p-value, cross-checked with the Mann–Kendall test on annual means. 1 DU = Dobson Unit.
